## Research Question

> Under what conditions do sacred political orders become pathological rather than corrigible, restrained, or transformable?

## Why It Matters

This question keeps PPW focused on explaining variation rather than proving a favored theory. It asks why sacred political orders sometimes escalate into pathology, but in other settings remain corrigible, restrain sacrifice, collapse, or transform into successor orders.

### `gtpp-crisis-outcomes-vary`

**Rival hypotheses:**

- Material capacity alone explains escalation, collapse, or restraint.
- Regime type alone explains outcome variation without sacred-political mechanisms.
- External military pressure explains outcomes without internal correction capacity.

**Rival hypotheses:**

- Political pathology is reducible to observed violence or coercion.

- Successor-order transformation is better explained by elite bargaining alone.
